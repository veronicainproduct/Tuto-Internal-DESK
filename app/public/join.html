Join TUTO Desk

Our team's to-do app. Two taps: sign in with Google, then allow notifications.

Open TUTO Desk
1

Sign in

  1. Open the invite link Veronica sent you.
  2. Tap Continue with Google and pick your work Google account.
  3. If Google says "Google hasn't verified this app", tap Advanced, then Go to TUTO Desk. It's our own team app.
  4. Leave the calendar box ticked and tap Continue. Your calls then show up by themselves.
2

Turn on notifications

Android or computer
  1. Tap Turn on on the orange card.
  2. Tap Allow.
iPhone
  1. Open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Open TUTO Desk from the new icon and sign in.
  3. Tap Turn on, then Allow.

That's it. You'll get a notification 15 minutes before calls, when you're made SPOC or @tagged, and a short summary each morning.

3

How to use it

Team / PersonalTeam tasks are seen by everyone. Personal tasks are seen only by you.
+Add a task. Pick a category; Personal keeps it private.
@riyaTag someone. The first person tagged becomes the SPOC, and they get a nudge.
fri 5pmSet a due date and time: today, tomorrow, 26 sep, in 3 days.
!highMark it High priority.
○Tap the circle to complete a task. Tap the task itself to edit, comment or delete it.
BellEvery nudge sent to you.
