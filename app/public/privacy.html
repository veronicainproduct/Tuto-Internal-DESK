Privacy policy

TUTO Desk is a private task app used by a small internal team. Last updated 24 September 2026.

Who runs it

TUTO Desk is run by Veronica Singh. Contact: veronica.singh2525@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • From your Google account when you sign in: your name, email address and profile picture.
  • From your Google Calendar, read-only, if you allow it: your events' titles, times and attendee emails.
  • What you enter: tasks, comments and settings.
  • From your device, if you turn on notifications: a push address your browser creates, used only to deliver reminders.

How it's used

  • Calendar events are used only to show you your own calls in the app and to send you reminders (for example, 15 minutes before a call). Other team members never see your calls.
  • Your name and email identify you to your team, for task assignment and @tags.
  • Your push address is used only to send you TUTO Desk reminders.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a private database on Cloudflare, in an account run by the team owner. As its administrator, the owner can technically access this data, including the calendar access you grant. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Reminders are delivered through your browser's own push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla).

Google user data

TUTO Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect your calendar at any time: in the app, go to Settings → My calls → Disconnect, or remove TUTO Desk at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have your data deleted, email the address above.
