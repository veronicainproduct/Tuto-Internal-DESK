  3. 3

    • Send me the Client ID; it isn't secret. For the Client secret, click the command I give you in the terminal and paste it there, so it never goes through chat.

    You · 1 minSign in and invite

    Teammates open the link, tap Continue with Google, and tap Turn on. Their calendar and reminders then work automatically. iPhone users first add the app to their Home Screen; the app shows them how.
